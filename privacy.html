JARVIS Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

What JARVIS accesses

With the owner's explicit consent, JARVIS reads Gmail message metadata and content, sends email only after the owner confirms each message, and reads and adds Google Calendar events.

How data is handled

JARVIS runs only on the owner's own computer. Google sign-in tokens are stored in a private file on that computer. Email and calendar data is not sent to any server operated by the developer, is not sold or shared, and is not used for advertising. Parts of an email may be passed to the AI model the owner has configured, only when the owner asks JARVIS to read or summarise it.

Google API Services

JARVIS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The owner can disconnect an account in JARVIS settings or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions at any time.
